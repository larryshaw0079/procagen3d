radius with 8 bolts on a bolt circle. The shoulder pivot sits at 40.5 cm
height and the elbow pivot at 86.5 cm (measured along the arm at rest,
pointing up). Base yaw, shoulder and elbow are revolute joints with
physically sensible limits; include a simple two-finger gripper at the
wrist. Painted steel body, dark rubber cable sleeves.
